SparSpan / Miniature rigging takeoff

Free view-only sample · fictional model millimeters · works offline
This is the free, view-only GitHub demo v1.0.0. Explore the fictional Cedar Gull sample and grouped cut totals. Editing, new projects, JSON save/reopen, CSV/SVG export, and the dedicated print report are unavailable in this build. A separate free SparSpan Lite v1.1.0 ZIP supports limited numeric edits to one fixed example (10 fixed anchors and 7 fixed paths); anchor IDs/names and route definitions stay fixed, with no project creation, save, or export. For custom projects and the full export/print toolkit, see the paid SparSpan edition on Gumroad. This view-only demo works offline; the paid-edition link opens only when selected.

04 · Measured takeoff report

Straight segments between points only. Curves, hardware and knots are not calculated. You must choose allowances and recheck the route physically.

Rounding: 0.01 mm per cut, midpoint ties away from zero; multiply rounded cut lengths by copies and add their rounded row totals. The displayed geometric path values are informational.

Before you cut

Measure anchors consistently, review paths, and save JSON before closing. There is no autosave.

Round each per-piece cut to 0.01 mm (half ties away from zero); multiply by copies, then sum rounded rows into group and grand totals. Group totals are not spool-packing optimization or material purchasing advice.
